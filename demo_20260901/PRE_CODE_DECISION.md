# PRE-CODE DECISION — Shelley’s Poochie Parlour – Stamford Limited

Status: `PRE-CODE GATE: PENDING INDEPENDENT REVIEW`. Local concept only; the user has authorised making the Demo, not deployment, customer contact, publication, or source transfer.

## Design Read and dials

**Design Read:** brand-register local dog-grooming page for cautious Stamford owners; an upbeat, assured grooming studio rather than a soft generic pet boutique.

- `SOUL: Graphic Grooming Studio` — assertive black/white substrate, sharp photographic crops, trademark fuchsia as the single accent, friendly rounded display texture.
- `SPECTACLE: 3/10` — deliberately restrained: a compositional CSS “cutout frame” hero, not automatic animation. The priority is factual clarity and the real official gallery.
- `DENSITY: 6/10` — enough service/care/contact specificity to make the purchase thesis tangible, without a live booking catalogue or price table.
- Anti-default: reject the category reflex of pastel mint, paw-print patterns and soft rounded cards. Also reject the current dense collage hero that loses Stamford-specific meaning.

## Premium substrate and layout diversification

- Palette lock: `ink #171616`, `paper #f7f2ed`, `berry #e90070`, `pink-light #ffb8d4`, `cyan #82d9ee`. The accent stays berry/pink; cyan is a background support colour, never a CTA accent.
- Type plan: locally loaded system display fallback stack with tightly tracked heavy headlines; clean humanist body. No generic Google-font dependency or faux luxury serif.
- Physical layer: subtle SVG grain, rounded cutout photo frame, translucent off-white borders and berry-tinted shadow. No pure black/white, gradients-as-decoration, or glass cards.
- Layout families: editorial hero split; asymmetric service image grid; wide statement/care band; contact ticket; full-bleed gallery strip; closing split.

## Current Offer Purchase Thesis

Target Market: United Kingdom. Current Offer Currency: GBP. Current Offer Price: `£99 AS-SHOWN HANDOVER / £199 ONE PERSONALISED UPDATE`.

The business owner can recognise a complete, Stamford-specific site because it brings their official dog imagery, care approach, local telephone/address and existing Fresha appointment route into one readable, responsive commercial path. It is not a minor styling patch: the proposed hierarchy changes a generic two-location collage entry into a clear service, trust and booking-handoff story. The later page will use only real, recorded assets/facts and will not claim booking results or copy platform reviews.
